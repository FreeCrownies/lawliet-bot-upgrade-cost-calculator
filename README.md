# Lawliet Fishery Calculator

Calculates the effect and the upgrade costs of the fishery tools of the Lawliet Bot:
Fishing Robot (Daily), Fishing Net (Voice), Fishing Rod (Message), Work and Metal Detector (Treasure).

Pick a tool, enter the level you are at and the level you want to reach, and you get

- what the **upgrade costs** in total,
- how much the **effect increases** (from → to),
- the effect, cost and total cost at the target level,

plus charts that show how all of this develops over the levels.

Have a prestige level? Enter it and all prices are adjusted (25 % cheaper per level).

**What can I afford?** Enter your coins (for example `2500000` or `2.5b`) and the calculator shows
how many levels you can upgrade from your current level, what it costs and how many coins are left.
